Now that I have outlined the next steps, I would like to start a new series of posts. In these posts, which I have called **Laboratory Notes**, I will discuss topics that I am studying in research papers and books. They will serve as small previews of future publications that I may eventually release on some of these subjects. The purpose is to provide a form of self-reflection while also allowing anyone interested to follow how the studies and research were carried out, as well as to learn about my perspective in a more informal way.

I believe this will make it easier for me to share my work with everyone. I hope you enjoy this type of publication.
